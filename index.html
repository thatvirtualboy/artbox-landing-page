A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Need a hand? Say hello.

Is Artbox free?+

Artbox is free to download and includes basic features. Artbox+ adds premium features through a subscription or lifetime purchase. You can see current options and pricing in the app. Printed photo books are purchased separately.

Is it just for drawings?+

Not at all. Save paintings, sculptures, building projects, school crafts, and anything else you can photograph. If they made it, there’s a place for it in Artbox.

Which devices can I use?+

Artbox is available for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 16.6 or later. Some features require newer software or compatible hardware. The App Store has the latest compatibility details.

Can I share with my family?+

Yes. Artbox+ users can share artist profiles with other Artbox+ users through iCloud sharing. You can also export saved projects to share with family.
